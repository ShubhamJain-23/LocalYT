# LocalYT Keyboard Shortcuts

The video player supports the following keyboard shortcuts (similar to YouTube):

## Playback Controls
- **`Space`** or **`k`** : Play / Pause
- **`m`** : Toggle Mute
- **`f`** : Toggle Full Screen

## Seeking (Skip Forward/Backward)
- **`←` (Left Arrow)** : Skip backward 5 seconds
- **`→` (Right Arrow)** : Skip forward 5 seconds
- **`j`** : Skip backward 10 seconds
- **`l`** : Skip forward 10 seconds

## Volume Controls
- **`↑` (Up Arrow)** : Increase volume by 5%
- **`↓` (Down Arrow)** : Decrease volume by 5%

## Playback Speed
- **`Shift` + `>` (or `.`)** : Increase playback speed by 0.25x (up to 4.0x)
- **`Shift` + `<` (or `,`)** : Decrease playback speed by 0.25x (down to 0.25x)
